Social Media Do’s and Don’ts for College Students

 Do’s
	•	Think before you post
Whatever you upload can stay online for a long time, so it’s better to pause and check if it’s appropriate.
	•	Keep your profile clean and professional
You don’t have to be too serious, but avoid posting anything that could create a bad impression later.
	•	Share useful or positive content
Posting about your learning, projects, or achievements can actually help you build a good image.
	•	Respect others online
Just like in real life, be polite and avoid unnecessary arguments or offensive comments.
	•	Check information before sharing
Not everything on the internet is true, so it’s important to verify before forwarding or posting.

⸻
 Don’ts
	•	Don’t overshare personal information
Avoid sharing things like phone numbers, location, or private details publicly.
	•	Don’t post in anger or frustration
Emotional posts can create problems later, so it’s better to stay calm before posting anything.
	•	Don’t copy content without credit
Always give proper credit if you are using someone else’s work.
	•	Don’t pretend to be someone you’re not
Showing fake skills or achievements might look good temporarily but can create issues later.
	•	Don’t ignore privacy settings
Make sure your accounts are secure and only visible to the right audience.
