# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

1. Always verify links before clicking, especially those received through emails, SMS, or WhatsApp.
2. Do not share OTPs, passwords, or personal details with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank or company.
3. Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts, and update them regularly.
4. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) for important accounts like email, banking, and social media.
5. Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions or accessing sensitive information.
6. Be careful while downloading apps; only install from trusted sources like Google Play Store or App Store.
7. Regularly update your phone and apps to protect against security vulnerabilities.

# UPI / Financial Safety

8. Never approve unknown UPI payment requests or scan QR codes sent by strangers.
9. Double-check UPI IDs and receiver details before sending money.
10. Do not click on fake “refund” or “cashback” links asking for bank details.

# Reporting Cybercrime

If you become a victim of cybercrime:
- Report immediately at: https://cybercrime.gov.in  
- Call Helpline: 1930

Staying alert and cautious can prevent most cybercrimes. Small habits can make a big difference.
